Who We Are

A Passion for Mehandi Art

Stainy Henna was born from a deep love for the ancient art of mehandi. What started as a childhood fascination with intricate henna patterns has blossomed into a professional studio dedicated to creating extraordinary designs for life's most beautiful moments.

Our team of skilled artists brings years of experience and an eye for detail to every design. Whether it's an elaborate bridal mehandi, a fun party design, or a simple festive pattern, we pour our heart into every stroke.

We believe that mehandi is more than decoration — it's a celebration of culture, love, and beauty. Every design we create tells a unique story, your story.
